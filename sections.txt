# Lane Runner sections — hand-made stretches of track (see CONTEXT.md and ADR 0001).
#
# Read each picture like looking down the track: the BOTTOM line is closest to you.
# Three columns = left, middle, right lane. One line = 5 metres of track.
#
#   .   empty                                  c   coin
#   L   low barrier (jump over)                ^   high coin (jump to collect)
#   O   overhead bar (roll under)              L^  low barrier with a high coin above
#   B   train (go round; stacked B's = one long train)
#   R   ramp up onto the train on the line above it (same lane)
#   M   moving train: drives toward you (stacked M's = one long moving train). Never has a ramp,
#       and nothing may sit in its lane above it in the same section.
#   Bc  coin on a train roof     B^  high coin above a roof     Rc  coin on a ramp
#
# Rules (the tests check these): every section starts and ends with an empty line, every line
# has exactly 3 cells, a ramp always has a train right above it, and no line blocks all three lanes with B.
